Computer Vision Researcher

I am a computer vision researcher with a broad interest in applying deep learning to real-world visual perception problems. My core focus is depth perception, including monocular estimation, stereo matching, and multi-view geometry, but I am equally drawn to related problems where the goal is the same: making vision systems more robust and reliable outside controlled conditions. This has led me to explore image restoration (super-resolution, dehazing, deblurring, denoising), segmentation, and unsupervised learning as complementary directions.

I place a strong emphasis on writing clean, efficient code that makes full use of the available hardware. My approach stays close to the engineering side of research: evaluating whether methods that perform well on paper hold up once they need to be deployed. My longer-term goal is to build practical tools for fields such as autonomous systems and medical imaging, work that remains useful outside the lab.

SLiDE: Stereo-LiDAR Depth Estimation with Domain-Aware Attention

Achieved state-of-the-art results in depth completion by fusing stereo RGB imagery with sparse LiDAR inputs. By effectively combining these modalities, the model outperforms standard monocular-based benchmarks on the KITTI dataset, providing significantly more accurate and dense depth maps.
